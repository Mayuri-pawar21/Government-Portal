Create a high-quality, government-grade web portal titled:

"AI-Based Image Forgery Detection and Verification System for Government Welfare Schemes"

Objective of the System:
This portal is designed to support government authorities in verifying images submitted by applicants for welfare schemes. The system uses AI-based image processing and detection techniques to identify forged, manipulated, or AI-generated images that may be used to misuse government welfare portals. The system provides risk-based assessment only and does not replace human decision-making.

Intended Users:
Government verification officers and authorized officials.

System Philosophy:
• AI-assisted, not AI-controlled
• Decision support, not automated approval
• Transparency and responsible use of AI

------------------------------------
Title:
Government Welfare Image Verification Portal

Subtitle:
AI-Assisted Image Authenticity Verification System

Description:
"Government welfare schemes rely on image submissions as proof of eligibility, damage assessment, or verification. However, misuse of such portals through forged, manipulated, or AI-generated images can mislead authorities and impact genuine beneficiaries. This portal applies AI-based image detection techniques to assist officials in identifying suspicious image submissions and improving the integrity of welfare distribution."

Key Highlights (Bullet Points):
• Detects forged, manipulated, or AI-generated images
